# Minecraft Datapack for Create thr Merguez modpack

I'm making this datapack for my modpack based on Create. It mostly add crafts using mods in the modpack.

## Recipes
### Campfire Cooking
 + `Logs` can be turned into `Ash` from the Supplementaries mod by placing them on a `Campfire`.

### Shaped Crafting
 + 2 `Barrels` can be crafted with 4 `Logs` in a diamond shape *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.
 + 4 `Chests` can be crafted with 8 `Logs` in a O shape *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.
 + `Debug Stick` can be crafted with 1 `Stick` and 2 `Cogwheels` form Create.
 + `Hopper` can be crafted with 2 `Logs` instead of a `Chest` *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.
 + `Budding Amethyst` can be crafted with `Amethyst Shards`, `Emeralds` and `Diamonds`. It then can be automated with Create.
 + `Name Tags` can be crafted with `Strings` and `Paper` *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.
 + `Repeater` can be crafted replacing `Redstone Torches` by their craft *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.
 + `Sponge` can be crafted from `Strings`, `Yellow Wool` and `Bucket`.

### Shapeless Crafting
 + `Clay Blocks` can be directly used in a crafting table to obtain `Clay Balls` instead of placing them and breaking them *(this may be moved to a vanilla minecraft datapack in the future)*.

### Smoking
 + Using a `Mechanical Fan` with a `Campfire` on any `Log` will turn into 2 `Ashes` from the supplementaries mod.
 + Using a `Mechanical Fan` witn a `Campfire` on `Mud` will turn it into `Dirt`.

### Splashing / Bulk Washing
 + Spraying `Water` on `Dirt` with a `Mechanical Fan` will turn it into `Mud Blocks`.
